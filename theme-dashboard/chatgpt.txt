Theme ABC Dashboard machine-readable endpoints

Primary snapshot:
./data/chatgpt_snapshot.json

Split endpoints:
./data/top10.json
./data/a.json
./data/b.json
./data/c.json
./data/standalone.json
./data/update_status.json

Schema notes:
- market_date: Tokyo Stock Exchange market date represented by the snapshot.
- updated_at: generation timestamp in Asia/Tokyo.
- quality.certified=true is required before using the snapshot for analysis.
- top10: current 124-theme score ranks 1-10.
- A: current Top10 and previous trading day also Top10.
- B: current rank 11-30 and Top10 at least once in prior 5 trading days.
- C: current rank 31+ and Top10 at least once in prior 5 trading days.
- theme_index: lookup table for all currently eligible themes, including themes outside A/B/C/TOP10.
- drivers: top five constituent stocks by deterministic within-theme driver score.
- standalone: stocks rising independently while broad theme rises are excluded.

When answering from this data, always state market_date and verify quality.certified first.
